Trick-Taking Card Game
Phase: Setup | Round: 1/13

Game Rules

Basic rules:
Four players, trick taking game.
Each player has a hand comprised of two sections, the Hidden and the Shown.
Only a player can see their Hidden hand. All Shown hands are visible to all players.

Cards played from the Hidden hand are scored as follows during the play:
2-10: face value
J: 11, Q: 12, K: 13, A: 14

Cards played from the Shown hand are scored as follows during the play:
2-10: face value + 2
J: 13, Q: 14, K: 5, A: 10

All cards are scored at face value (J=11, Q=12, K=13, A=14) during the final tally.

Play is as follows:
Each player is dealt 13 cards from a standard 52 card deck into their Hidden hand.
Each round proceeds as follows:
- Reveal: The leader chooses another player. A random card from that player's Hidden hand is placed face up into that player's Shown hand.
- Play: Players take turns placing a card from their hand (Hidden or Shown) into the Play zone, starting with the leader.
- Determination: Whoever has the highest scoring card in the Play zone wins the round. They discard the card they played, take the remaining card with the highest face value as a prize, and discard the rest.

Edge Cases & Tie Breaking:
- If two cards tie in play score, those cards are discarded before choosing a winner. This applies to any two cards that tie in score, even if they aren't the highest.
- If three or four cards tie in play score, all cards are discarded and nobody wins.
- If potential prize cards tie in face value, they are also discarded and the next highest is taken.
- The leader for the next round is the player counter-clockwise from the previous trick's winner. If there was no winner, the leader does not change.
- You must choose a player with cards in their Hidden hand to reveal a card. If all players are out of Hidden cards, no cards are revealed that round.
