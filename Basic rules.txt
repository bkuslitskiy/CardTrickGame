Basic rules:
Four players, trick taking game.
Each player has a hand comprised of two sections, the Hidden and the Shown.
	Only a player can see their Hidden hand. All Shown hands are visible to all players.


Cards played from the Hidden hand have a Value during play of:
2-10, face value.
J, 11
Q, 12
K, 13
A, 14

Cards played from the Shown hand have a Value during play of:
2-10, face value + 2.
J, 13
Q, 14
K, 5
A, 10

All cards have a Score equal to their face value (J11, Q12, K13, A14) for prizes and the final tally.



Play is as follows:

Each player is dealt 13 cards from a standard 52 card deck into their Hidden hand.

Each round proceeds as follows:
	Reveal:
		Player 1 chooses another player. A random card from that player's Hidden hand is then chosen and placed face up into that player's Shown hand.
	Play:
		Player 1 places a card from their hand (Hidden or Shown) into the Play zone, starting the play.
		Player 2 places a card from their hand (Hidden or Shown) into the Play zone.
		Player 3 places a card from their hand (Hidden or Shown) into the Play zone.
		Player 4 places a card from their hand (Hidden or Shown) into the Play zone.
	Determination:
		Determination resolves in four steps, in this order. Ties are settled twice: once on Value, before the winner is known, and once on Score, after the winner is known but before the prize is awarded.

		1. Value ties (before the winner is determined):
			Compare the Value of all four cards in the Play zone.
			Every card that shares its Value with another card is discarded, whatever the size of the tie. This applies to any tie in Value, even one that isn't for the highest Value.
			Two cards tied -> both discarded, two cards remain.
			Three cards tied -> all three discarded, one card remains.
			Four cards tied, or two separate two-way ties -> every card is discarded and nothing remains.
		2. Winner:
			Whoever played the highest-Value card still remaining wins the round.
			If nothing remains (a four-way tie, or two two-way ties), nobody wins the round and all four cards are discarded.
		3. Score ties (after the winner is determined, before the prize is awarded):
			The winner discards the card they played. The cards still remaining are the prize candidates.
			Compare the Score of the prize candidates. Every candidate that shares its Score with another candidate is discarded, and the next highest is taken.
			If the three tied cards were removed in step 1, the winner has no candidates left: they win the round and take no prize.
		4. Prize:
			The winner takes the remaining candidate with the highest Score into their scoring zone.
			Any card left over is discarded.

Each round is led by the player one seat clockwise from the previous round's leader. If you're doing it correctly, the previous round's leader should be last to go in this round, and whoever went second last round leads this one.
	This happens every round no matter what the outcome was: who won the round, and whether a prize was taken, have no effect on who leads next. Over four rounds every player leads exactly once.

Each player tallies up the cards they won, highest score wins.


Edge cases:
	You must choose a player with cards in their Hidden hand to reveal a card.
		If a player has no Hidden cards left, you must choose a different player.
			If all players are out of Hidden cards, no cards are revealed that round.
